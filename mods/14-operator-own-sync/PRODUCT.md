# Operator sync when the IDE is closed

06 timers the **architect** home because `projectPath` has no watcher. The operator corpus usually has `serve --mcp` while the IDE is open. Close the lid and that watcher dies. The unattended loop still needs a graph.

This is a user-bus timer that calls `codegraph-as-owner.sh` (mod 02) as the **owner UID**. It will not `pkill` a live serve. It will not sync as the architect.

What this will not do: enable linger on your machine. Copy the units yourself.

Ten-minute outcome: templates under the operator `.horizon/systemd/`.
