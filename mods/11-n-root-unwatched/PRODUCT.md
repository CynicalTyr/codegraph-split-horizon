# N roots, one serve

00 taught two graphs. Real boxes grow a third: a docs tree, a second app, a LAN config checkout. Colby's 1.6.0 still has **one** `serve --mcp --path`. Everything else is `projectPath`. Those extras have **no watcher**.

Ordinary CodeGraph users `init` the folder they opened. Split-horizon users pick a primary corpus and list the rest. `codegraph init /` is how you eat the machine. Indexing a CIFS/NFS share of another computer is how you index someone else's disk (mod 13).

What this will not do: start N MCP servers. That fights Colby's daemon and your WAL.

Ten-minute outcome: `NROOT.md` in the operator `.horizon/` directory. You fill in your extra `--path` names. Serve stays pinned to one root.
